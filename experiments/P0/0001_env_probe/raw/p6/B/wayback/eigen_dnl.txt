 Predicting Depth, Surface Normals and Semantic Labels with a Common Multi-Scale Convolutional Architecture Predicting Depth, Surface Normals and Semantic Labels with a Common Multi-Scale Convolutional Architecture David Eigen Rob Fergus {deigen,fergus} @ cs.n yu.edu [LINK:http://web.archive.org/web/20221010101350/http://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.4734v4.pdf] Paper PDF (ICCV 2015) Paper PDF (ICCV15 Version) --> We address three different computer vision tasks using a single basic architecture: depth prediction, surface normal estimation, and semantic labeling. We use a multiscale convolutional network that is able to adapt easily to each task using only small modifications, regressing from the input image to the output map directly. Our method progressively refines predictions using a sequence of scales, and captures many image details without any superpixels or low-level segmentation. We achieve state-of-the-art performance on benchmarks for all three tasks. We also recently submitted depth and normals predictions from a slightly earlier version of our system in the [LINK:http://web.archive.org/web/20221010101350/http://cs.nyu.edu/~silberman/rmrc2014/] Reconstruction Meets Recognition Challenge (RMRC) at ECCV 2014.
